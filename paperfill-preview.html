📖 Official Document Guide & FAQs

Property Transfer Forms & Guidelines

Complete information on official Punjab CVT-1, Federal FBR IT-5, and DHA Lahore Dealer Performa documents for property buyers, sellers, and estate agencies.

🏛️

What is CVT-1 (Punjab Capital Value Tax)?

Punjab Government • Revenue Department

CVT-1 is the official Capital Value Tax declaration form mandated by the Government of Punjab for all real estate and immovable property transfers. It formally records the transaction value, plot or house identification (phase, sector, plot number), and the identities of both the seller and purchaser along with their Computerized National Identity Cards (CNIC).

In P Fill, you can generate an accurate, publication-grade Punjab CVT-1 PDF filled directly with official coordinate alignments, ready for immediate signature and submission to revenue authorities.

📋

What is IT-5 (FBR Advance Income Tax)?

Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) • Sections 236K & 236C

IT-5 is the official Advance Income Tax withholding statement required by Pakistan's Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 (specifically Section 236C for sellers and Section 236K for purchasers). It certifies tax collection at the time of property transfer or registration.

P Fill prepares and populates the official IT-5 PDF form with seller, purchaser, CNIC, and property particulars, eliminating manual handwriting and potential administrative clerical errors.

🏢

What is the Dealer Performa?

DHA Lahore • Estate Agency Transfer Representation

The Dealer Performa is the official representation slip utilized during property transfer submissions in the Defence Housing Authority (DHA Lahore). It records the concise property code (in the standardized Phase-Sector-Plot format such as 7-Q-123), the seller's full legal name, CNIC, and city location, the purchaser's legal name, CNIC, and city location, as well as the authorized Estate Agent (EA) names and contact phone numbers for both parties.

With P Fill, estate agents and consultants can instantly fill, preview in high-resolution, print, or download completed Dealer Performa documents with bold typography and official formatting.

100% Client-Side • Zero Cloud Storage

P Fill operates with a strict 100% client-side privacy architecture. All PDF rendering, OCR processing, and document population are computed directly within your local web browser. No property deeds, CNIC numbers, addresses, customer records, or financial information are ever saved or transmitted to external servers.
