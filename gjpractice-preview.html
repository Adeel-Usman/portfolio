1 Authentic ISSB Psychological Modules

⚡

Word Association Test (WAT)

Evaluates spontaneous subconscious reactions and reveals candidate temperament, character, and mental conditioning.

  • Format: 100 consecutive words projected one-by-one.
  • Timing: 10 seconds per word (ISSB standard), plus 13s Beginner and 7s Fast Drill modes.
  • Word Bank: 15 standard sets (1,500 words) + 2 dedicated Negative Word sets (200 words) to master handling pressure-inducing stimuli.
🖼️

Picture Story Test (TAT / PPDT)

Thematic Apperception Test designed to assess projective storytelling, environmental perception, empathy, and leadership initiative.

  • Format: 4 stories per session chosen from 48 authentic ISSB sketch images (12 standard sets + Final Practice).
  • Phase 1: 30 seconds of observation with circular countdown timer.
  • Phase 2: 3 minutes 30 seconds of focused story composition following Past (Background), Present Action, and Constructive Resolution.
✍️

Pointer Story Test (Sentence Starter)

Incomplete situation starters testing crisis intervention, moral courage, resourcefulness, and emotional composure under pressure.

  • Format: 2 situations per session selected from 26 authentic ISSB starters across 13 sets.
  • Phase 1: 30 seconds to assimilate the starter crisis prompt.
  • Phase 2: 3 minutes 30 seconds to construct an assertive narrative where the protagonist resolves the crisis proactively.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I handle negative words in WAT?

Remember that WAT is a Word Association Test, not a sentence-making test. In ISSB, the time is very short (only 10 seconds per word), so you must write short associative responses of only 2 to 3 words. Do not avoid or deny negative words (avoid using words like "not" or "never"). Instead, associate them with a positive or constructive action in just 2 to 3 words (e.g., for Defeat: "Accept and improve" or "Overcome with effort"; for Failure: "Teaches lesson" or "Step to success").

Why does the picture disappear after 30 seconds?

At the actual ISSB, the sketch is projected for exactly 30 seconds on the screen and removed. Candidates must write their story from memory and impression.

Are my typed answers stored online?

No. GJ Practice operates entirely in your client-side browser sandbox. Your responses are strictly private and never uploaded to any remote server.
